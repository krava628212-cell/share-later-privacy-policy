Privacy Policy for Share Later

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Last updated: November 18, 2026

Share Later helps you save a link or text and reminds you to share it later.

Developer: Share Later app owner.
Privacy contact: krava628212@gmail.com.

Local processing

The app has no registration, accounts, own server, advertising analytics, or cloud synchronization. Links, reminders, recipients, and settings are stored locally in SQLite on your device.

Data used by the app

  • links and text received through Android Share or entered manually;
  • reminder content, times, status, selected app, and recipient;
  • recipient name, phone number, Telegram username, or Instagram username when saved by you;
  • language, theme, and delay settings;
  • the local notification-permission state;
  • a link temporarily placed in the clipboard before opening Instagram.

The app does not request or process passwords, messenger tokens, chat contents, photos, videos, microphone data, location data, or payment information.

Device contacts

If you explicitly choose contact search, Android may provide access through READ_CONTACTS. Contacts are used only to display a matching name and number and fill in the recipient form. They are not uploaded, sold, shared with the developer, or used for advertising. You can deny or revoke this permission in Android settings.

Notifications

Reminders use Android local notifications and are scheduled on the device. Without notification permission, records may still be saved locally but notifications will not be shown.

Third-party apps

When you open a reminder, the link or text is passed to WhatsApp, Telegram, or Instagram through standard Android intents, links, and clipboard operations. Those apps process the data under their own privacy policies. Share Later cannot read chats or verify that a message was sent; you confirm the final send action yourself.

Deleting data

You can delete reminders and saved recipients in the app. Clear the app data or uninstall the app in Android settings to remove all local data. There is no account or server-side copy.

Security, children, and changes

Data is stored in the app's private Android storage. Share Later is not specifically directed to children. If data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email krava628212@gmail.com or visit the developer's GitHub profile.
